Danyal Mohiuddin

Founder & CEO · SaleCompass (SMC-Private) Limited

During my postgraduate studies at the University of Northampton, UK, I kept encountering the same problem: SMEs had no reliable way of knowing what was actually driving their revenue. That gap became my obsession and the foundation of everything I have built since.

Background

Danyal Mohiuddin is an AI researcher whose work has been published in the proceedings of the IEEE International Conference on AI Innovations and Industry (ICAIII 2026) in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

doi.org/10.1109/ICAIII69475.2026.11521642 ↗

He has also presented at the 8th International Conference on Education and Technology (ICET 2026), organised with Curtin University, Australia, alongside institutions across Türkiye and Ukraine. He has authored several peer-reviewed papers on AI and revenue analytics.

What began as a research question became a product. SaleCompass is a proprietary AI platform that connects every touchpoint to real revenue outcomes, built specifically for SMEs who have never had access to this kind of intelligence before.
