International Conference Appearances

IEEE International Conference on AI Innovations and Industry (ICAIII 2026)

Jeddah, Saudi ArabiaIEEE2026

Presented original research titled "A Systematic Literature Review of Multi-Channel Analytics Linked to POS and Connected to Food Business in the UK" at the IEEE International Conference on AI Innovations and Industry, held in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organisation, with conferences recognised globally as the benchmark for engineering and technology research.

The paper proposed an integrated AI-based analytics framework addressing a critical and underserved gap in how UK businesses connect digital marketing activity to real revenue outcomes. It was presented before an international audience of AI researchers, engineers, and industry practitioners. The paper has been formally published in IEEE proceedings.

UK University Repository

Officially Archived in the Ravensbourne University London Research Repository

A co-authored paper with Dr. Naureen Farhan, Course Leader for Computing & Emerging Technologies at Ravensbourne University London, has been formally deposited in the university's official research repository, part of the GuildHE Research network supported by the University of London.

This represents institutional recognition that goes beyond journal publication: the research has been formally accepted, archived, and made publicly accessible through a UK University's academic infrastructure. The paper is filed under the Department of Computing & Emerging Technologies, placing it within the university's official body of academic knowledge.

Academic Supervisor

Ayesha Anees Zaveri

PhD Researcher; Former Lecturer, Universiti Kuala Lumpur (UniKL), Malaysia (2022–2025). Specialises in AI, Software Engineering, Intelligent Systems & Recommendation Agents.

My collaboration with Ms. Ayesha Anees Zaveri led to the co-authored paper "A Systematic Literature Review of Multi-Channel Analytics Linked to POS and Connected to Food Businesses in the UK." The research tackled a real and pressing challenge, how UK food businesses integrate data across multiple channels. Drawing on Zaveri's expertise in intelligent systems and AI-driven software, we developed an integrated framework that brings these data streams together, offering a practical and academically grounded solution. The paper was presented and published in the proceedings of the IEEE International Conference on AI Innovations and Industry (ICAIII 2026), Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, an internationally recognised, Scopus and Web of Science indexed venue.

AI Beyond Academia

Published Op-Ed: The Real AI Skills Gap

The Frontier Post

I have written an op-ed titled "The Real AI Skills Gap", which has been published on The Frontier Post, a Pakistani English-language platform having an established presence in Washington D.C., USA. It was published on 5th July 2026.

The article argues that it is increasingly important to equip younger generations with essential digital competencies to thrive in the changing landscape of education, business and research in the age of AI.

As someone who has taught AI in universities and community settings, I have argued that the onus of spreading AI knowledge should not be confined to the workplace but also should reach the classrooms and community centres.
