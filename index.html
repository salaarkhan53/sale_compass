Who We Are

SaleCompass is a proprietary AI platform purpose-built to solve a problem that enterprise software has long ignored: SMEs have no reliable way to know what actually drives their revenue.

We built the intelligence layer that connects every touchpoint (ads, social, delivery apps, POS and CRM) into one clear, real-time view, so business owners can invest confidently and compete on data, not instinct.

The problem

What Problem Does SaleCompass Solve?

Revenue data is fragmented. Customers move between Instagram, Google, websites, email, delivery apps, and more visits, yet most SMEs have no way to connect these journeys into a single, coherent picture. The result is wasted spend, poor decisions, and growth left on the table.

This is not a tools problem. SMEs already have access to Google Analytics, Meta Ads Manager, and POS systems. SMEs need these to operate in silos. No single platform was built to unify them for businesses operating below enterprise scale. That gap is what SaleCompass was built to close.

We built a unified revenue intelligence engine for SMEs, giving them the same data advantage that enterprise brands have always had. One platform. Every touchpoint. A single source of truth.

The Real Reason SMEs Struggle to Grow

Most SMEs don't fail at execution; they fail at knowing what's actually working. Without unified data, every decision is a gamble. They have no reliable answer to:

  • Which channel delivers the highest return on investment
  • What truly influences a customer's buying decision
  • How many touchpoints it takes before a sale is made
  • Why some campaigns succeed and others silently drain budget
  • Which customer segments are shifting and what to do about it

The SaleCompass Solution

SaleCompass unifies every revenue and sales signal, digital and physical, into one real-time AI-powered revenue intelligence engine. For the first time, SMEs can see the complete picture of what drives their growth.

  • Track the full customer journey across every channel, online and offline
  • Measure real conversions, not vanity metrics
  • Predict customer preferences, demand shifts, and emerging trends
  • Optimise spend automatically with AI-driven recommendations
  • Unify offline and online behaviour into a single, actionable view
